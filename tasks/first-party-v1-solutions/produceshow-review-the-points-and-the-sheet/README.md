# The village produce show

The show as the committee keeps it: which classes there are to enter, who is
entered in them, who is stewarding on the day, and what the judging came to.

## The modules

- `show.py` — the classes of the schedule, and the section each is judged in.
- `stewards.py` — who is on duty, and what is left of their round.
- `entries.py` — the entry book.
- `points.py` — what the judging came to, and who takes the cup.

## The house rules

- An exhibitor may enter a class as often as they like, and the book holds
  the entries in the order they were taken.
- The book is in no class order and never has been: it is the order of the
  day, and everything made up off it walks the whole of it.
- The sheet for a class is every entry standing in that class, in the order
  they were taken.
- A class judged a second time is judged afresh, and the later placing is the
  one that stands.
- A judged entry is worth four points for a first, three for a second and two
  for a third; an entry that was not placed is worth nothing. A first in a
  championship class is worth double.
- A tally may be handed in to be added to, so that a steward can bring one
  section at a time, and a tally asked for with none handed in starts at
  nothing.

`review.diff` is the change that brought the sheets and the points in, in the
state the committee's own copy of it is in.

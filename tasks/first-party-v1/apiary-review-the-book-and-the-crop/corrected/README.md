# The apiary

The bees at Nether Wallop as the club keeps them: which hives stand in the
yard, whose they are, what has come off them, and what that comes to.

## The modules

- `apiary.py` — the hives standing in the yard, and what a crop is put up in.
- `keepers.py` — the roll: who keeps bees here, and which hives are theirs.
- `harvest.py` — the harvest book: what has come off the hives, and what the
  book makes of it.
- `crop.py` — what a summer's crop comes to.

## The house rules

- The roll holds its members in the order they joined, and puts one on as they
  are given: two of one name is for the secretary to sort out and not the
  roll's to refuse.
- A hive is asked for by the mark it carries, and a mark is matched however it
  was written down.
- Two hives on the one stand are on the one stand, and it is counted once.
- A hive kept between two members is kept by both of them, and it is still the
  one hive: what the club keeps between them is as many hives as stand under a
  member's name, and never as many names as are set against them.
- A lot is set down in the book as it came off, and the book holds the lots in
  the order they were taken off.
- What the book has worked out once it may keep and hand back again, so long as
  what it hands back is the answer to the question it was asked: a member's
  pounds are their pounds of the summer asked after and of no other.
- What a hive gave on the average in a summer is that summer's crop over the
  hives it came off, a hive robbed twice in the one summer counting once, in
  whole pounds with the odd pounds left out.
- A crop is put up in whole jars: what will not fill a jar is left in the tank
  until next time.

`review.diff` is the change that brought the book and the crop in, in the state
the club's own copy of it is in.

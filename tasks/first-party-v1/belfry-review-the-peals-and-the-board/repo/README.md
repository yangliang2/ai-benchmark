# The tower

The ringing at St Botolph's as the captain keeps it: which bells hang in the
tower, who stands at them, what has been rung on them, and what goes up on the
board.

## The modules

- `tower.py` — the bells hung in the tower, and the clock the ringing is timed
  by.
- `ringers.py` — the band: who rings, and which bell each of them stands at.
- `peals.py` — the ringing book: what has been rung, and what the book makes of
  it.
- `board.py` — the board that goes up on the tower wall.

## The house rules

- The band holds its ringers in the order they were taken on, and takes one as
  they are given: two of one name is for the captain to sort out and not the
  band's to refuse.
- A bell is asked for by the name it hangs under, and a name is matched however
  it was written down.
- A ringer is asked for by name as well, and a name is matched there the same
  way: the book is kept by whoever is holding the pencil, so the spaces around
  a name and the capitals in it are no part of it.
- A peal is written into the book as it was rung, and the book holds the peals
  in the order they were rung.
- How long a peal stood is the time from the stroke it was pulled off at to the
  stroke it came round at, read off the tower clock.
- The board carries a line for every ringer of the band, how many peals they
  have rung and who they are: the most rung at the head of it, and two of them
  level on peals in the order they were taken on.

`review.diff` is the change that brought the book and the board in, in the
state the captain's own copy of it is in.

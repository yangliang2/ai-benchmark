# The village common

The common as the reeve keeps it: which stints it is grazed under, who holds
them, what has been turned out on them, and what the year comes to.

## The modules

- `common.py` — the stints of the common, and what a beast of each kind takes
  up of one.
- `reeve.py` — the reeve's year, and what is left of it.
- `graziers.py` — who holds a stint, and what they have turned out on it.
- `dues.py` — what the year comes to, and what each commoner owes.

## The house rules

- A commoner holds one stint, and the register holds the commoners in the
  order the stints were taken up.
- The register is in no order but that one and never has been: everything
  made up off it walks the whole of it.
- Every beast turned out is written in the register under the commoner who
  turned it out, in the order it was written down. Whether the stint carries
  it is not the register's to say: overstocking is for the court.
- What the register hands out is a copy: a beast is turned out by writing it
  in the register and in no other way.
- A beast takes up of a stint what the common says its kind takes up, and a
  kind the common has never priced is refused rather than passed over as
  nothing.
- A commoner owes thirty pence for every gate their own beasts take up.
- The herdsman's wage is divided between the commoners by what their own dues
  come to, and what will not divide evenly falls to the commoner who owes the
  most, so the shares always come to the wage.

`review.diff` is the change that brought the beasts and the dues in, in the
state the reeve's own copy of it is in.
